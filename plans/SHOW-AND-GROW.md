# Show & Grow — Phase 1
Local rollout order: apply the approved MVP schema, then show-and-grow.sql to isolated D1. Both are draft migrations outside automated deployment. No existing projects or media are published automatically. Retain the existing production audience and leave CODEMATE_ENABLED disabled until schema and approved administrator grants are in place.

Showcase uses a separate allowlisted projection and media route. A project manager saves a private draft, then explicitly publishes a chosen build to signed-in CodeMate accounts. Unlisting, moderation, archive and blocking also gate media. Source project tasks, chat, Commit links and account names do not enter the exhibition response. External preview hosting has its own access rules; do not publish preview links containing personal data. Contact filtering is a limited heuristic, not an anonymization guarantee.

Phase 2 (community feedback to task and comeback) and Phase 3 (recommendations and team requests) are separate. No production data, roles or visibility were changed.
